                                    About this data

                                    Chrome history
                                    Page addresses, saved titles and visit times from the date range you choose. Sample results are always labeled.
                                    Active time
                                    Approximate time on the active website while Chrome is focused and your device is active.
                                    Trails
                                    Pages, notes and checkpoints you deliberately save. These stay until you edit or delete the Trail.
                                    Hidden and excluded sites
                                    Hidden sites are left out of reports. Excluded sites are not used for future time collection or reminders.
                                    Storage
                                    Daytrail keeps settings, Trails and measured activity in this Chrome profile. It does not send browsing history to a Daytrail server.
