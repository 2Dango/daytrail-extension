              Google account

              Set up a Google OAuth client once for this copy of the extension. Then you can connect your Google account here.

              1. In Google Auth Platform, open or create a project and complete its Branding and Audience settings.
              2. Under Clients, create a Chrome Extension client. Use the extension ID below as its Item ID. Open Google Auth Platform

              Replace the existing manifest.json in the extension folder with the downloaded file, then reload Daytrail at chrome://extensions.

              About this data

              Google searches
              Visits to Google search results with a search term. A returning visit can count again. Reloads are skipped.
              Unique sites and favorites
              Sites are grouped by hostname, with and without www combined. Favorites are the five sites with the most visits.
              Data processed
              The number of web visits analyzed in the selected date range, including search result visits. Reloads, subframe visits and Chrome’s generated keyword records are skipped.
              History connected
              The first time you granted history access for this connection. The date range can include visits you made before connecting.
              Privacy and history coverage
              Google verifies your identity. Chrome grants access to the history saved in this profile. Daytrail reads it when you view the dashboard and keeps your history on this device. Only your connected account details and approval date are saved by Daytrail. Google My Activity is outside this dashboard’s history source.
